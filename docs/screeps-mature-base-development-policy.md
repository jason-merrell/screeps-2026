# Screeps World Mature Base Development Policy

- Policy ID: `2026.08.31-Screeps World Mature Base Development Policy v1`
- Revision: 1
- Effective date: 2026-08-31
- Requirement locator: `§ Binding obligation`
- FSPM governance baseline: `Namauu/governance-docs@02d581886a759d19044ff91a80d743fa042f23f7`

## Purpose

This source-controlled policy is the immutable Requirement Source for the
definition-only mature-base General Project candidate. It establishes one
bounded development obligation for an owned Screeps World colony. The project
authority package must store `§ Binding obligation` as the first line of
Requirement Verbiage and transcribe the obligation below verbatim on the next
line; it may not move the locator into a parallel record field, or broaden,
narrow, summarize, or silently revise the obligation.

## Binding obligation

> Each owned Screeps World colony shall be developed through an accepted deterministic RCL8 room-development plan and independently evidenced Bootstrap, Logistics, Core Economy, Advanced Operations, and Mature RCL8 base states before the temporary development Project is complete. Controller level is an eligibility condition only and shall not constitute evidence that a base state has been achieved.

## Scope boundary

The obligation governs the temporary endeavor that produces the room plan and
the five independently evidenced base states. It ends only after the integrated
mature outcome has been independently accepted under a future governed
lifecycle.

Ongoing infrastructure maintenance, repair, routine energy service, standing
defense, and later strategic re-planning are continuous Colony Portfolio work.
They are not silently added to the temporary Project and do not extend its
scope indefinitely.

## Evidence boundary

Controller level is prerequisite evidence, never achievement evidence. An RCL8
controller cannot by itself satisfy a stage, produce a Deliverable receipt, or
complete the Project. Final mature-base evidence must prove all of the
following independently:

- an accepted, deterministic version-4 RCL8 blueprint;
- realized logistics at the governed service thresholds;
- every required mature capability is authorized, implemented, and operating;
  and
- defensive geometry and operational readiness meet their governed thresholds.

Construction sites, intended commands, planned structures, prepared but
unauthorized capabilities, and the present state of a live room are not
receipts. Evidence must be captured by the named responsible principal in the
specified system of record and decided independently under the applicable
Receipt Validation contract.

An evaluator or capability-manifest symbol name is only an integration locator,
not an immutable semantic binding. Before activation, the applicable evaluator
contracts and mature-capability manifest must be bound to reviewed content so
later source changes cannot silently alter the meaning of accepted evidence.

Revision 1 has not yet authorized or bound that durable append-only system of
record. Its candidate Receipt Validation contract must remain explicitly
blocked; mutable live Memory and caller-supplied hashes are not substitutes.

## Definition-only authority boundary

Revision 1 is a source for an inert, source-controlled authority definition.
It does not activate or persist a Project, Requirement, Deliverable, Task,
Activity, receipt, decision, or completion state in Screeps Memory. It creates
no retroactive evidence for colonies that already exist. Authorization,
activation, evidence capture, acceptance, lifecycle closure, and durable
history require later explicitly governed changes.

The candidate package uses a source-controlled Screeps service principal while
the runtime lacks canonical human Employee, Position, OU, and ARCI records. The
service principal is not a human Accountable or approver, and its attestation
must never be represented as a human signature. That parity gap remains tracked
by issue #164.
